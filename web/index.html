One craft = 3 doses; doses are fungible (2×(3) → (4)+(2)), so per-dose value = (sell(4)+sell(2))/6. Profit/craft = doses × (sell(4)+sell(2))/6 − (craft cost − 10%·goggles × secondary cost), where doses = 3 + 15%·amulet. Potions that always make 4 doses (antidote+, antidote++, anti-venom+, super combat, super antifire) sell as a (4) and aren't amulet-boosted; guthix rest always makes 3; the amulet does not work on components (haemostatic poultice, weapon poison); goggles don't save serum 207 ashes. Sell price is after the 2% Grand Exchange tax (capped at 5M). GP/h = profit/craft × crafts/hour; crafting speed from the OSRS wiki (2 ticks/craft, 1 tick = 0.6 s → 3000 crafts/h; Guthix rest 1 tick → 6000). Untradeable potions (Surge, Sanfew) can't be priced and are excluded from rankings.
